Our Story

Your Gateway to Northern Pakistan's Hidden Treasures

Awan Tours has been crafting unforgettable adventures in Northern Pakistan for over 20 years. Starting with a small group of passionate mountain enthusiasts, we've grown into a trusted name in luxury tourism. Our mission is to showcase the breathtaking beauty of Hunza Valley, Skardu, and Gilgit to travelers worldwide while preserving the rich cultural heritage of these magnificent regions.

Premium accommodations in carefully selected hotels
